Privacy Policy

Terrest · Effective 14 November 2026

Terrest is a tabletop role-playing game that runs as an Activity inside Discord. This policy explains what information Terrest handles when you play, why, and how long it is kept. Terrest is a small hobby project. It has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sells your information.

Information we receive from Discord

When you launch Terrest, Discord asks you to authorize it with the identify permission. Terrest then receives:

  • your Discord user ID, username and display name, and
  • your avatar (an image reference that Discord hosts).

Terrest does not receive your email address, your messages in Discord, your server or friend lists, or anything else from your Discord account.

Discord also gives Terrest an access token. The server uses it only to confirm who you are with Discord. It is not stored. To stop players joining tables that aren't theirs, the server also asks Discord which users are connected to your Activity session. That list is held in memory for a few minutes at most and is never stored.

Information you create while playing

  • Game content: game settings, character names, stats, resources, equipment and inventory, dice rolls, and the table log.
  • Images you upload: character portraits and scene backgrounds. Images are shrunk and re-encoded, and the server removes embedded metadata (such as camera details and GPS location) before anyone else sees them.
  • Chat messages: public messages are shown to everyone at the table. Private messages are shown only to the sender and the people they chose.

Who can see your information

Terrest is a shared game, so the people at your table see your display name, avatar, character and public messages. The storyteller can also see items that players have pocketed, as part of the game. Private messages reach only the sender and the recipients, not other players and not the storyteller unless they are a recipient.

As the operator of the service, the developer can technically access stored game data, but does not look at it except when needed to keep the service running, fix problems or respond to abuse reports.

How long information is kept

Each table's data (members, characters, uploaded images, the table log and chat) is stored so a game survives restarts. A table and everything in it is deleted automatically 30 minutes after its last player leaves. The storyteller can also reset a game at any time, which clears its settings, characters and log.

Our hosting provider keeps short-lived technical logs (such as request times, IP addresses and error messages) for troubleshooting, and deletes them on its own schedule, typically within days.

Service providers

  • Discord provides sign-in and runs the Activity. Your use of Discord is covered by Discord's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare hosts Terrest's server and stores table data. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Information is shared with these providers only as needed to run Terrest. It is not shared with anyone else, except where required by law.

Cookies and browser storage

Terrest does not use cookies. It saves one display preference (which creature appears at the table) in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device.

Your choices

  • You can stop using Terrest at any time. Leaving the table starts its 30-minute deletion countdown once everyone has gone.
  • You can remove Terrest's access to your Discord account in Discord under User Settings → Authorized Apps.
  • For any privacy question or request, such as deleting data from a table that is still active, contact us as shown below.

Children

Terrest is available only to people who meet Discord's minimum age requirement in their country. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
